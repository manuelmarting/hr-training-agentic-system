# SOP-SAF-01 — Personal Protective Equipment & Manual Handling

**Site:** All warehouse operations | **Applies to:** All warehouse operatives
**Regulatory basis:** Ley 31/1995 de Prevención de Riesgos Laborales, Art. 19 (worker information and training duty); RD 486/1997 (workplace safety conditions)
**Version:** 2.1 | **Effective:** 2026-01-15 | **Review cycle:** Annual

## 1. Purpose

Defines mandatory PPE by zone and safe manual handling technique for warehouse operatives. Non-compliance is a stop-work condition.

## 2. PPE requirements by zone

| Zone | Mandatory PPE |
|---|---|
| General warehouse floor | Safety boots (S3 rated), hi-vis vest |
| MHE (mechanical handling equipment) lanes | Safety boots, hi-vis vest, hard hat where overhead racking exceeds 4m |
| Battery charging bay | Face shield, acid-resistant apron, safety boots |
| Dangerous goods segregation area | Safety boots, hi-vis vest, nitrile gloves |
| Cold chain / chiller | Thermal jacket, non-slip boots rated for wet/cold surfaces |

PPE is issued at induction and reissued on damage or loss — report immediately to your shift supervisor. Operating in any zone without the mandatory PPE for that zone is a reportable near-miss even if no injury occurs.

## 3. Manual handling — load assessment and base of support

Before lifting any load:

1. Assess the load: weight, size, shape, grip points, and whether the load is stable or shifting.
2. Check the route: clear of obstructions, adequate lighting, no wet or uneven flooring.
3. Establish a stable base of support: feet shoulder-width apart, one foot slightly forward, close to the load.
4. Bend at the knees, not the waist. Keep the load close to the body. Keep the back straight, avoid twisting while lifting.

Loads over 25kg for a single operative, or any load that cannot be safely gripped with a stable base of support, require either mechanical assistance (PPT/reach truck) or a team lift (see §4).

## 4. Safe lift execution — heavy or awkward loads

For loads between 15–25kg, or awkwardly shaped loads under 25kg (long, unbalanced, or with no clear grip points):

1. Perform the load assessment in §3 first.
2. Use a hook grip and lift in stages: floor to knee, knee to carrying position — do not lift directly from floor to carrying height in one motion.
3. Never lift and twist simultaneously; reposition your feet instead.
4. If at any point the load feels unstable or the operative feels unable to maintain the base of support from §3, stop and call for a team lift.

## 5. Team lift coordination

For loads exceeding single-operative limits, or when §4's stop condition is triggered:

1. A minimum of two operatives; one is designated lift lead.
2. Lift lead gives verbal count ("ready — brace — lift") audible to all participants before any movement begins.
3. All participants lift and set down in unison, on the lift lead's verbal signal only.
4. Communication must be verbal, not assumed — a nod or eye contact is not a valid signal under this procedure.

## 6. Common errors

Operatives frequently underestimate load weight from packaging size alone rather than checking the manifest or label — this is the leading cause of unassessed lifts. Always confirm weight from documentation before applying the load assessment in §3, not from visual estimate alone.
